Website Plan

Site Name

Name: Streaming Series Hub

Logo: 📺 Streaming Series Hub

Reason: The name is simple and easy to remember. It clearly communicates that the website is a central place (“hub”) for television series recommendations from popular streaming platforms.

Optional domain: streamingserieshub.com (example only)

Site Purpose

Streaming Series Hub provides curated lists of recommended television series from Netflix, Disney+, Prime Video, HBO Max, and Apple TV+. The site helps users quickly discover series by browsing top picks, using a simple search/filter, and submitting suggestions through a form. The goal is to make it easy for users to find new series to watch based on their preferences and the latest trends in streaming content.

Scenarios

  • “What are the top recommended Netflix series for someone who likes drama?”
  • “How can I quickly find science fiction series on Prime Video?”
  • “Where can I submit a series suggestion to be added to the site?”
